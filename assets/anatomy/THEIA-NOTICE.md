# THEIA anatomy attribution
Source: https://github.com/hpfrei/body-anatomy-3d-viewer
Anatomical geometry derives from Z-Anatomy. Runtime and model are distributed under CC BY-SA 4.0; see LICENSE and upstream README.
THEIA modifications: local Streamlit selection messages, mesh identifier preservation, session-specific messaging and removal of global selection writes. These adaptations are also CC BY-SA 4.0.
Only public runtime assets are vendored. OHIF (MIT) and openAnatomy remain separate local repositories and are not republished here.

THEIA 4 additions: cyan holographic materials, wireframe visualization, lab control styling and original procedural orbital scene. These assets and adaptations are also provided under CC BY-SA 4.0. No reference-image portraits, mission logos or third-party spacecraft geometry are included.

THEIA 4.1 additions: exact-identifier system filtering, bidirectional keyboard/model selection, smooth camera transitions and isolation state handling. These adaptations retain CC BY-SA 4.0. The 826 model identifiers and geometry are unchanged.
